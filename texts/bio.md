# Bio / 简介

Murphy Nile (Ziling Zhou) is a transmedia artist working with electronic media to construct dystopian digital landscapes. His work explores causality, prediction, geopolitical histories, and technological imaginaries, asking how technical systems shape perception and produce new forms of alienation.

He was a finalist for the 2025 Lumen Prize Experiential Award with *Control Club* and was nominated for the UNESCO City of Media Arts Award at the Independent Days International Film Festival with *CONTROL*. His work has been exhibited and screened at ISEA, ACM SIGGRAPH DAC, Milan Machinima Festival, SUPERBOOTH 24, and West Bund Museum, among others.

## 简介

Murphy Nile（周子凌）是一位跨媒介艺术家，以电子媒介构建反乌托邦数字景观。他的创作涉及因果关系、预测、地缘政治历史与技术想象，关注技术系统如何改变感知并产生新的异化形式。

《Control Club》入围 2025 年 Lumen Prize 体验类奖项，《CONTROL》获德国独立电影节 UNESCO 媒体艺术之城奖提名。他的作品曾在 ISEA、ACM SIGGRAPH DAC、米兰 Machinima 电影节、SUPERBOOTH 24 和西岸美术馆等展映。

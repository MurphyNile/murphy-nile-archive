# Low Latency Engine

2026 · Audiovisual Live Performance

Searchlights, route displays, and industrial sound evoke a surveillance console whose infrastructure increasingly fails to coordinate. The work premiered at Cedar Land, Shanghai, in WAVES CROSS Vol. 3 / LIGHT SAMPLING.

探照灯、路线显示与工业声音构成一套监控界面，城市设施却逐渐失去协调。作品在上海 Cedar Land 的 WAVES CROSS Vol. 3 / LIGHT SAMPLING 首演。

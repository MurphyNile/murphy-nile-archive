# Missing Signals

2026 · Audiovisual / Expanded Animation

Across two curved screens, an aircraft becomes progressively harder to track while a second interface keeps updating its schedules and administrative records. Shown at Loading...Project 4, UFO Terminal, Shanghai.

在两块弧形屏幕上，一架飞机逐渐脱离追踪，另一块屏幕却仍在更新航班时刻与行政记录。作品展出于上海 UFO Terminal 的 Loading...Project 4。

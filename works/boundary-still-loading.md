# Boundary Still Loading

2026 · Machinima / AI Film

Based on the coast of Chongming Island, a patrol machine leaves its assigned route and enters an egret grove. Changing scans and conflicting rules bring it to the edge of what its map can explain.

取材于崇明岛海岸。一台巡逻机器偏离既定路线，进入一片白鹭栖息地。不断变化的扫描和互相冲突的规则，把它带到地图所能解释的边界。

[Project page](https://murphynile.com/work/boundary-still-loading)

The work was shown through Coastal Co-Art and ACM SIGGRAPH DAC Urban Digital Canvas Shanghai and selected for screening at the 2026 China Computational Art Conference. [SIGGRAPH DAC exhibition record](https://dac.siggraph.org/artwork/boundary-still-loading/)
